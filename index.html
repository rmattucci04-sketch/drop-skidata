drop

Your ski days, in 3D.

drop records your runs, lifts, speed and vertical with the phone in your pocket. Explore every piste of thousands of ski areas, plan your day lift by lift, follow famous circuits like the Sellaronda turn by turn, and share your day as an Instagram story.

  • Free, no account, no ads.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone.
  • Maps from OpenStreetMap, 3D terrain for the Alps and Europe’s big resorts.
