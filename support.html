Help and questions

Something not working, or an idea? Write to support.dropski@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, the iOS version and the drop version (Settings in the app).

Recording a day

How do I record?

Tap “Start your day” when you arrive, put the phone in your pocket and ski. drop splits your day into runs and lifts on its own. You can pause at lunch and resume later; at the end, hold “Finish”.

It stopped recording when the screen locked

Open iOS Settings › drop › Location and choose “Always”. With “While Using” iOS stops the GPS when the screen is off. Also check that Low Power Mode is off.

How much battery does it use?

GPS uses battery, especially in the cold. A full day typically uses a good part of a charge. Keep the phone in an inside pocket and carry a small power bank on long days.

My speed or vertical look wrong

Measurements come from GPS and the barometer and depend on the signal. Deep valleys, tunnels and gondolas can briefly confuse the GPS. Pauses and long GPS gaps are not counted as runs.

Maps, plans and navigation

A piste or lift is missing or wrong

Maps come from OpenStreetMap, edited by volunteers. You can fix it there (or report it); the change reaches drop with a later data update.

Does drop work without signal?

Recording works with no signal at all: GPS does not need the mobile network. Ski areas you have opened once are saved on your phone, so their maps, plans and navigation keep working up the mountain. Open your ski area once before you go, on Wi-Fi or with signal.

Can I trust the directions?

Use them as a guide. Pistes and lifts can be closed and the data can be out of date. Always follow the signs, the piste markers and the ski patrol.

Your data

Everything you record stays on your iPhone; drop has no account. To erase it, open Settings in the app › “Delete all my data”, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.
